Section 03 · Problem

Ads and the CA Campaign

Problem

Strong campaigns use the weak campaigns' money

  • Google can spend up to 2× a campaign's daily budget on a busy day (₹500 can become ₹1,000)
  • All campaigns share one prepaid balance, so the strong campaign uses it up first
  • When the balance runs out, every campaign stops:
    • October: Google Ads ran on only 17 of 31 days
    • 15–19 Nov: no FB leads at all, because the Meta campaigns showed a "payment error"
Problem

FB ads are reaching the wrong parents

  • In November, only 2 of 15 FB leads the team reached went well
  • Most said they wanted home tuition, lived too far, or found the fees too high
Problem

The CA campaign brings low or no leads

  • It gets little budget after the stronger campaigns have spent theirs
  • CA isn't on page 1 organically either
  • Other possible causes: the landing page, strong competitors (ArivuPro, Yeshas), keyword settings, timing around CA exam registration

Section 05 · Problem

Guest Posting Is Slow and Complicated

What we want from it: a do-follow backlink, which is a link from another website that Google counts as a vote for us. Many sites only give "nofollow" links, which carry little ranking value.

What one guest post takes

1

Find a relevant site

2

Email the editor with a topic pitch

3

Wait for a reply

Most don't reply, so it can take 10–20 emails to get one yes

4

Agree the topic

Some sites charge a fee

5

Write an original 800–1,500 word article

6

Submit and wait days or weeks to go live

7

Check the link is live and do-follow

So 20 a day isn't possible
  • 20 a day is about 500 original articles a month, plus thousands of emails
  • Google treats mass guest posting as link spam, which can get links ignored or hurt rankings
Section 06 · Solution

Lead Management App with AI WhatsApp

1. Lead comes in

Form or WhatsApp

2. AI WhatsApp chats

Instantly confirms whether they want a call

3. Team notified

Lead appears in the app for all sales members

4. Claim & call in 1 min

Otherwise it goes back for others

5. Status updated

After every call

  What the team gets

  • Every lead, with the AI chat summary, in one place
  • Leads in the app, or on WhatsApp groups for those who prefer it
  • No lead waits: if the person who claimed it doesn't call, someone else can take it

  What management gets

  • See every AI chat live
  • See who called which lead, how fast, and the result
  • Real admission numbers, by source and by team member

  It also makes our ads smarter

  • Every visit, demo or admission marked by sales is passed to Meta and Google Ads automatically
  • The ads learn which parents actually join and show ads to more people like them
  • Better-quality leads and a lower cost per lead over time

Section 07 · Solutions

Solutions: Ads, SEO and GBP

  Ads

  • Fund consistently: top up monthly or switch to automatic payments, so ads never stop
  • A separate budget for each priority line
  • Target a radius around the 4 branches
  • Ad text says "classes at our centres or live online, no home tuition"
  • Know which ad each lead came from

  SEO and content

  • Fix technical issues: duplicate homepage, web. subdomain, old PGCET PDF, overlapping RMS page
  • Merge extra Navodaya pages (a typo URL and old landing pages) into the main page
  • Push keywords close to page 1: CET, CA, Banking, GATE, NDA
  • Build missing pages: State board, IGCSE, PU Commerce, CLAT, CUET, KPTCL, Navodaya class 6/9
  • 30+ quality posts per category
  • Backlinks from directories and selected guest posts

  GBP

  • 2 posts a week per branch, 8 a week across all 4
  • Post types: batch dates, results, demo classes, photos, admissions
  • Every week: ask for reviews, answer Q&A, add photos
  • Focus on Malleshwaram and Koramangala

  Team and tools needed

5 interns/writers (2 content · 1 GBP · 1 outreach and backlinks · 1 ads and lead tracking) · Semrush · backlink budget
